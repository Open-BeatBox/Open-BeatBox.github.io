# Modules

## Feeder

The feeder module handles reward delivery and pellet interaction. Current assembly notes include motor/rotor fit checks, sensor board placement, connector seating, and physical removal of a small black locating pin when it prevents flat sensor contact.

## Screen

The screen module has an order-sensitive assembly:

1. Screw the screen alone into the first housing part.
2. Place and route cables.
3. Connect the two parts.
4. Screw the second housing part after cables are seated and strain-free.

Optimal cable length still needs formal definition.

## Tunnel

The tunnel module supports movement or task-zone transitions depending on the configured protocol.

## Lighting

The ceiling / light module integrates white and IR illumination for day/night monitoring.
